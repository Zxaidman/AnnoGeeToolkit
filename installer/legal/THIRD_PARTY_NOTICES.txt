PYREVIT
=======

pyRevit is a separate third-party framework and prerequisite used to host the
current AnonGee Toolkit extension. pyRevit is not owned by Zxaidman and is
licensed under its own terms, including GPLv3 for the pyRevit project.

Installer milestone 0.3.0 detects an existing pyRevit installation but does
not automatically download or install pyRevit. If a later installer bundles
or redistributes the official pyRevit installer, the applicable pyRevit
license and notices must be distributed with that prerequisite.

RELEASE CHECKLIST
=================

Before each production release:

1. Compare this inventory with tools/requirements.txt.
2. Confirm the packaged versions match the pinned requirements.
3. Preserve upstream LICENSE/COPYING/NOTICE files present in wheel metadata.
4. Review native-library notices for NumPy, Shapely, pywin32, and any newly
   added binary dependencies.
5. If pyRevit or another prerequisite is bundled, include its full license and
   required notices separately.
6. Re-review xlwings usage to confirm no PRO-only feature is being packaged or
   relied upon without the appropriate license.

This notice is an engineering license inventory, not a substitute for legal
review before large-scale commercial distribution.
